# Design Thinking methods

Use these methods selectively rather than producing every artifact.

- Empathize: interviews, observation, stakeholder map, jobs-to-be-done, evidence inventory.
- Define: persona, journey map, point-of-view statement, How Might We questions, scope and non-goals.
- Ideate: divergent options, impact-effort matrix, desirability-feasibility-viability-risk comparison.
- Prototype: storyboard, paper prototype, wireframe, service blueprint, technical spike.
- Test: task script, think-aloud, observation notes, success measures, continue/pivot/stop decision.

Core references: Brown (2008), Stanford d.school Design Thinking Bootleg, and Design Council Double Diamond.
