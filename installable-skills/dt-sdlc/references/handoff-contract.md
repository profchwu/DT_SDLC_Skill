# DT to SDLC handoff contract

The handoff is ready only when all items exist:

- target users and operating context;
- evidence-backed problem statement;
- required inputs, processing or decisions, and outputs;
- AI role, authority boundaries, and human decisions when applicable;
- approved MVP and explicit non-goals;
- intended delivery context or an explicit decision to defer it;
- constraints, risks, assumptions, and dependencies;
- measurable success criteria;
- acceptance examples;
- unresolved questions with owners or decision dates;
- explicit user approval status.

Do not convert an unapproved idea into an implementation commitment.
